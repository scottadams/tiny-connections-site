Support

Tiny Connections · last updated 10 October 2026

Get in touch

Write to me@scottadams.co.

Someone is behaving badly

Open the small world you share, tap the round button at the top right to see who is in it, and tap the person. Choose Block, or Report.

A report goes to the person who runs Tiny Connections and blocks that person for you straight away. Reports are looked at within 24 hours.

Delete your account

On the home screen, tap the round button at the top right, then “delete account”. Your name, your face and your place in every small world are removed straight away.

If you no longer have the app, write to me@scottadams.co and say which name the account used and which small world it was in.

I am not told when someone opens

Check that notifications are allowed for Tiny Connections in your phone’s Settings, and that you have not muted the small world.

My phone does not ring when someone picks up my open

Open the app once after installing or updating it, so that it can say where your phone is to be rung. If it still does not ring, write to me@scottadams.co.

The call ended by itself

It is meant to. Every call lasts 60 seconds from when you are both connected, and then ends for both of you.
